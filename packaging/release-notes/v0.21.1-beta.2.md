ZapFast 0.21.1-beta.2 (Znga) adds Favourites on the rail, an on-device Insights dashboard, and Keep, which saves deleted and edited messages and every attachment on your computer.

## New

- **Favourites on the rail.** The heart opens your favourite chats and groups, the way Archived opens archived ones. Chats takes you back to every chat.
- **Insights.** The bar-chart button opens a dashboard of your WhatsApp activity, worked out on your computer: totals and how they changed, a daily trend, your busiest hours on a weekly heatmap, streaks, top chats with reply times, who starts conversations, what gets shared, and people you haven't talked with in a while. It uses when and with whom you talk, never what was said.
- **Words (optional).** Turn it on to see your most used words and emoji and how long your messages are. Text is read in memory only and dropped when you turn it off.
- **Import a chat export.** Analyse a WhatsApp "Export chat" `.txt` or `.zip` (Android or iPhone) without saving it.
- **Keep.** Messages deleted for everyone, and the versions that edits replace, stay in your local archive under Insights → Deleted & edited. Every attachment is saved as it arrives, and "Save everything now" fetches archived ones that are not on disk yet. Messages you delete yourself are not kept, and nothing about keeping reaches WhatsApp.

## Also

## Known limitations

- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click ZapFast and choose **Open** the first time.
- An attachment that was never downloaded before its sender deleted it cannot be recovered.
- Calls are still a work in progress, as in upstream 0.21.1.
