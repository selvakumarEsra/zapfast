ZapFast 0.21.1-znga.1 is ZapFast 0.21.1 with **Znga**, an opt-in AI companion that runs on your machine. Click the ✨ on the left rail to set it up.

## New

- **Alerts.** Messages that need you now (urgent questions, time-sensitive plans, someone writing repeatedly, mentions in groups) are scored on your machine without a model and raise a "Znga" notification.
- **Briefings.** "What happened / what needs you", on demand or after you have been away. You can also have them copied to your own "Message yourself" chat (off by default, at most 6 a day).
- **Digital twin.** Znga learns how you write and drafts replies in your voice for the chats you mark as Twin. It also suggests check-ins with close ones who have gone quiet, and turns "tell Mom and Alex I'm 20 min late" into one personal draft each. **It never sends to anyone but you without your click.**
- **Composer tools and Ask.** Suggest a reply; rewrite a message friendlier, shorter or more formal, or with its spelling fixed; translate; and ask questions about your recent chats.
- **Engines.** Your installed Claude Code (`claude -p`, which signs itself in; ZapFast never reads its token), Ollama for fully local use, or your own Claude, Gemini or OpenAI-compatible key.

## Privacy

Znga is off until you turn it on. Cloud engines ask for consent before reading anything. Locked and excluded chats are never read, and message text stays in memory.

## Updates

This build updates only from [selvakumarEsra/zapfast](https://github.com/selvakumarEsra/zapfast/releases), signed with the fork's own key, so an update never replaces Znga with upstream ZapFast.

## Known limitations

- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click ZapFast and choose **Open** the first time.
- Calls are still a work in progress, as in upstream 0.21.1.
