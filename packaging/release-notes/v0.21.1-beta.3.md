ZapFast 0.21.1-beta.3 (Znga) fixes Insights showing no data, and lets it cover your whole history: it shows how far back each chat goes, asks your phone for older messages, and merges chat exports into your numbers.

## Fixed

- **Insights showed no data.** It looked for the archive in the wrong folder; it now reads the archive of the account in the window. Forgetting a kept message under Deleted & edited works again for the same reason. Switching accounts never shows the previous account's numbers.

## New

- **History tab in Insights.** Every chat with how far back it goes and how many messages it has: **Full history**, **With export**, or **Partial**. The Overview says from which date the numbers run and how many chats are complete.
- **Fetch older history.** Tick chats (or the 10 busiest partial ones) and ZapFast asks your own phone for their older messages, the way scrolling to the top of a chat does: one request every 15 seconds, up to about 500 messages per chat per run, with progress and a Stop button. Nothing is sent to anyone else. On an iPhone, WhatsApp may show "Syncing" while it answers.
- **Merge a chat export.** After importing an export and choosing your name, pick the chat it belongs to (the matching one is offered first). Only the messages this computer does not have are added to the account's numbers, so merging the same export twice changes nothing. Merges last for the session, are never written to the archive, and can be removed. "Keep separate" works as before.

## Known limitations

- The Mac and Windows packages are not code-signed. On a Mac, right-click ZapFast and choose **Open** the first time.
- Merged exports are not remembered after ZapFast quits.
- An attachment that was never downloaded before its sender deleted it cannot be recovered.
- Calls are still a work in progress, as in upstream 0.21.1.
